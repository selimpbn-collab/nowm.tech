Mohit Maya EA

MT5 Expert Advisor for XAUUSD (gold) on hedging accounts. An averaging ladder that opens a sell, adds bigger sells as price rises against it, and closes the whole basket together when price falls back to one shared TP.

•Start: press START, opens SELL 0.01 at market

•Ladder: adds sell each time price rises one grid step above last entry (0.80 → 3.50)

•Lots: 0.01...0.11 linear (11 levels), then ~8% larger, capped at 1.00/order

•Exit: lot-weighted avg entry − 4.0 TP, all positions close same second

•Repeat: short cooldown, then new cycle

GoldLadderBasket EA

Dynamic martingale-style ladder with linear-then-geometric lot sizing and basket TP. Adds market orders while price moves against the basket, then closes all positions together when the target is hit.

•Entry: cycle starts with SELL 0.01 at market

•Ladder: GridStep(n) = min(BaseStep + (n-1)*StepGrowth, MaxStep)

•Lots: 0.01...0.11 linear, then ~8% larger each level, capped at 1.00

•Exit: close ALL positions together when floating profit >= BasketTP_Money

•Visuals: dashed TP line, live P/L labels for every entry
